# Investor-Ready Startup Intelligence Profile (End Output Specification)

## Objective

The platform should automatically generate a comprehensive, investor-ready startup profile from founder inputs, uploaded documents, website analysis, and AI-generated insights.

The final output should resemble a combination of:

* LinkedIn Company Page
* Pitch Deck
* Investor Data Room
* Startup Dashboard
* Growth Analytics Platform

The profile must allow investors to evaluate a startup without requiring multiple meetings or documents.
