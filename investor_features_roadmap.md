# BlackWhale Investor Platform: Future Features & Due Diligence Roadmap

This document outlines high-value features, visualization widgets, and due diligence modules recommended for implementation to make the BlackWhale startup profile platform fully complete for venture capital and angel due diligence.

---

## 1. Advanced Financial & Cap Table Simulators
* **Dynamic Dilution Calculator**: 
  - **Concept**: A slider-driven capitalization table interface.
  - **Functionality**: Allows investors to simulate the dilutive impact of their proposed investment round. VCs can input round size, pre-money valuation, and options pool expansions to see immediate post-money shareholding splits.
* **Burn Rate & Runway Scenario Simulator**: 
  - **Concept**: A financial planning sandbox.
  - **Functionality**: Founders and investors can adjust operational toggles (e.g., changing monthly headcount growth, marketing budgets, or hardware shipping speeds) to see how the cash runway changes in real-time.

---

## 2. Document Security & Investor Analytics (DRM)
* **Granular Data Room Access Control**: 
  - **Concept**: Secure vault permissions.
  - **Functionality**: Gatekeeps sensitive files (detailed tax audits, patent certificates, employment contracts) behind digital NDA signatures, with disable-print/disable-download watermarked PDF viewers.
* **Investor Engagement Heatmaps**: 
  - **Concept**: VC activity logging.
  - **Functionality**: Displays backend telemetry to the founders showing exactly which pages or slides of the pitch deck a prospective investor viewed, where they paused, and which documents they downloaded.

---

## 3. Customer Cohort & Unit Economics Ledgers
* **Net Revenue Retention (NRR) Cohort Table**: 
  - **Concept**: SaaS health visualization.
  - **Functionality**: A cohort table representing clinic client lifetime value. It shows logo churn vs expansion revenue over 12-month cohorts, proving product stickiness.
* **LTV/CAC Payback Period Visuals**: 
  - **Concept**: Acquisition efficiency graph.
  - **Functionality**: A chart plotting the cost of customer acquisition (CAC) against customer lifetime value (LTV) to highlight the exact month a customer becomes profitable (payback period).

---

## 4. Technical Competitor Battle Card
* **Interactive Comparison Matrix Grid**: 
  - **Concept**: Competitive landscape widget.
  - **Functionality**: A comprehensive comparison matrix comparing the startup against global players (e.g., Oqulix vs. MindMaze vs. XRHealth) across multiple parameters:
    - Target accuracy latency (ms)
    - Hardware dependency (PC vs. standalone VR)
    - Deployment site (Clinic vs. Remote Home Care)
    - Pricing model ($/month subscription vs. one-time lease)
    - FDA/Regulatory clearance status

---

## 5. Regulatory & Clinical Trial Pipeline Tracker
* **FDA/CE Compliance Checklist**: 
  - **Concept**: Auditable compliance workflow.
  - **Functionality**: A visual checklist showing regulatory filing phases (pre-submission, trial recruiting, validation audits, final filing, and approval). Clicking any phase displays the linked submission letters or ethical review documents.

---

## 6. Threaded Due Diligence Q&A Room
* **Searchable Due Diligence Q&A Logs**: 
  - **Concept**: Q&A thread.
  - **Functionality**: An interactive interface where prospective VCs can post questions about technology, financials, or clinical trial designs. Founders can reply directly, and can flag answers as public (visible to all VCs) or private (limited to the asking VC).
